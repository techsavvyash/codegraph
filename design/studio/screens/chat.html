What happens when a customer invoice is created?

The request lands in 1InvoiceController.create(), which validates the payload and delegates to 2InvoiceService.createInvoice(). Inside a single transaction the invoice and its line items are written, then 3StockLedgerService.decrementStock() reduces stock for each line and appends a stock-ledger entry. A PDF render job is queued last for email delivery — the customer-facing flow is described in 4User Guide › Creating Invoices.
